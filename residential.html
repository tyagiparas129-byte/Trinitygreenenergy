🏠 Rooftop solar for your home

A solar system on your roof produces electricity during the day and can reduce what you draw from the grid. We look at your roof, your bill and your family's usage first, then suggest a system that fits.

System sizes we install

Typical home rooftop systems come in these sizes. The right size depends on your roof area, shade and electricity use, so we confirm it at a site visit.

1 kW

A small system for modest daytime use or limited roof space.

2 kW

Suits many small homes with a few fans, lights, a fridge and TV.

3 kW

A common size for family homes with more appliances or air conditioning.

5 kW and larger

For bigger homes, heavier use, or when you want to add battery backup.

Check your roof

A quick checklist before we visit. If you are not sure about any item, that is fine. We will assess it with you.

  • Shadow-free roof area for most of the day (no tall buildings, trees or water tanks over the panels)
  • Strong, sound roof surface that can carry panels (RCC or sheet)
  • Roughly 8 to 10 sq. metres of space for each 1 kW of panels
  • A valid electricity connection in your name or your family's
  • Access to the roof for installation and later cleaning

Your monthly bill and savings

Solar can reduce your electricity bill, but how much depends on your usage, your tariff, system size and sunlight. The best starting point is your latest electricity bill.

Savings calculator

Government subsidy

The Government of India runs the PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana for residential rooftop solar. Support may be available for eligible households, but the amount and eligibility depend on the scheme rules at the time, your state and your electricity company (DISCOM).

  • Eligibility has included owning a house with a suitable roof, a valid electricity connection and no earlier solar subsidy
  • The scheme is planned to run until FY 2026-27, and applications go through the national portal
  • We can guide you through the process, but we cannot guarantee that you will qualify or the amount you will receive
Always confirm the latest rules on the official scheme page: PM Surya Ghar on india.gov.in.
