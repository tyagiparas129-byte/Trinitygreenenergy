🔋 Hybrid solar and battery storage

If power cuts affect your home or business, a battery can keep important loads running. We help you understand the options before you spend anything.

Three types of solar system

Each has a different purpose.

On-grid

Works together with the grid. No batteries. Usually the lowest cost. Stops producing for safety when the grid is off.

Off-grid

Not connected to the grid. Uses batteries for all power. Suited to places without reliable grid supply.

Hybrid

Connected to the grid and has batteries. Can supply selected loads during power cuts.

Battery options

The right battery depends on how much backup you want and your budget. We will explain the trade-offs in plain language.

Lead-acid

Lower upfront cost, shorter life, needs more upkeep.

Lithium-ion

Higher upfront cost, longer life, compact and low maintenance.

Plan your essential loads

A battery rarely runs everything. Choosing what matters most keeps the system affordable.

  • List what must keep running: lights, fans, fridge, router, a few sockets, a pump, workstations
  • Decide how many hours of backup you need
  • We calculate battery size and inverter capacity from that list
  • You can add more later if your needs change

Is it worth it for you?

That depends on how often and how long your power goes off, and what an outage costs you. We will talk it through honestly, including when on-grid alone is the better choice.
