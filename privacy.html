Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information TrinityGreen Energy collects through this website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. We try to keep it simple and to collect only what we need.

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, mobile number, email address and address, when you create an account. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We cannot see it.
  • Enquiries: your name, mobile number, email (optional), city, the solution you are interested in, your monthly electricity bill (optional), your preferred time to call, and any message you write.
  • Service requests: the type of help you need, your description, your preferred time to call, and your name and mobile number.
  • Project information: if you become our customer, details such as your project name, system size, project stage and notes from our team.
  • Solar system readings: if we connect your system's monitoring with your agreement, its generation readings.
  • Technical information: basic technical data such as your IP address and browser type, which is normally recorded by the services that run this website.

3. How we use it

  • To call, message or email you about your enquiry or service request.
  • To prepare proposals and quotes, and to design, install and maintain your solar system.
  • To show you your own enquiries, projects and system information when you are logged in.
  • To keep the website secure and working.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Where it is stored and who can see it

Your information is stored using Google Firebase (Google Cloud), and the website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Their servers may be located outside India. Only authorised TrinityGreen Energy staff can see customer records. Each customer can see only their own information when logged in.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only when needed:

  • With service providers who run this website and store the data (such as Google and GitHub).
  • With manufacturers, installers or other partners, only as needed to supply and service your system.
  • With government portals or electricity companies, when you ask us to help with applications or approvals such as a rooftop solar subsidy.
  • When the law requires it.

6. Browser storage and third parties

When you log in, your browser stores a login session so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising trackers. Our pages load some code libraries and fonts from public content delivery networks, which can see your IP address when they serve those files.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services, and meet legal, tax and accounting duties. After that we delete it or remove details that identify you.

8. Your choices

  • You can see and update your name, mobile number and address on your profile page when logged in.
  • You can ask us to correct or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, by writing to us at the email address above.
  • You can withdraw your agreement to be contacted at any time. This does not affect anything we did before.

We aim to follow the data protection laws that apply in India, and we will respond to requests within a reasonable time.

9. Security

We use Firebase access rules and login protection so that customers can see only their own records. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and log out on shared devices.

10. Children

This website is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your information? Write to trinitygreenenergy3@gmail.com or call 86079 71755.
