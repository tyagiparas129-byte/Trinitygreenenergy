🏢 Solar for your business

Daytime electricity use in shops, offices, schools and hotels often lines up well with solar generation. We study your bills and your building before we suggest a system.

Who it is for

Businesses that pay regular electricity bills and have usable roof or open space.

Shops and showrooms

Cooling, lighting and billing equipment run through the day.

Offices

Computers, air conditioning and lighting during working hours.

Schools and colleges

Strong daytime use across classrooms and labs.

Hotels and hospitals

High, steady use where backup power also matters.

How we plan it

We start from your numbers, not from a standard package.

  • Review of 6 to 12 months of electricity bills and your sanctioned load
  • Site visit to check roof area, shade and structure
  • Estimated monthly and annual generation, and a possible reduction in your bill
  • A return-on-investment and payback estimate, shown as a range with the assumptions stated

On-grid or hybrid

Most businesses choose between two set-ups.

On-grid

Connected to the grid. Uses solar when available and grid power otherwise. Simpler and lower cost, but it stops during a grid outage.

Hybrid with battery

Adds storage so selected loads can keep running during power cuts. Costs more and needs careful planning of what to back up.

Read more about hybrid and battery systems

A word on savings and payback

Savings and payback depend on your tariff, usage pattern, system cost, financing and any benefits that apply to your business. We will share an estimate after reviewing your actual bills, never before.

Please note: every building, bill and roof is different. We do not quote fixed savings, subsidy amounts or payback periods until we have checked your actual situation. Any figure you see on this site is an estimate only.
