🏭 Solar and EPC for industry

Factories and industrial sites use large amounts of power, so design matters. We handle the engineering, installation and grid connection, and plan around your load profile.

Types of installation

Chosen according to the space and structure you have.

Large rooftop

Panels on factory sheds and warehouses using existing roof space.

Ground-mounted

For open land at or near the plant when roofs are limited.

Hybrid or storage-supported

Where power quality or backup for critical processes is a concern.

How we size your system

Capacity is a recommendation based on data, which we confirm with you.

  • Study of your consumption and demand pattern, including time of day
  • Review of sanctioned load, tariff category and grid conditions
  • Site survey of roof strength, shading and available land
  • A recommended capacity with the reasoning shown

What we deliver (EPC)

One team from design to commissioning.

Engineering

System design, layout, drawings and equipment selection.

Procurement

Panels, inverters, structures and cabling matched to the design.

Installation

Mounting, wiring, earthing and safety work by trained crews.

Grid integration

Coordination for connection with your electricity company.

Monitoring

Tracking of generation after commissioning.

Operation and maintenance

Ongoing cleaning and service to keep output steady.

Costs and returns

Project cost, savings and payback depend on size, site, tariff and approvals. We prepare these figures after a site assessment and share the assumptions behind them.

Please note: every building, bill and roof is different. We do not quote fixed savings, subsidy amounts or payback periods until we have checked your actual situation. Any figure you see on this site is an estimate only.
